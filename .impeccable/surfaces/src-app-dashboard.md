# Reputra app: owner dashboard, checkout, admin

Mode: Operate. Audience: business owners on a phone for a quick look; the Reputra team on a laptop. Jobs: see whether customers went on to Google, read private notes, ask on WhatsApp, pay and renew; admin confirms payments, delivers setup packs, watches renewals.
Constraints: shares the website's palette and Geist; no stored customer contacts; Google policy honesty; owner chose "Setup path, then pulse" by delegation ("chose what you think is the best").

## Direction contract
THESIS: The home screen shows where a business stands, then what it earned: a launch track (paid, Google link, stand on the way, first customer) that folds into the 30-day result. It refuses the KPI-tile template and the welcome banner.
OWN-WORLD: Warm stone frame (#f5f4f0 family) holding sidebar and top bar as one L; white sheet with 1px #e8e7e3 edge, 16px radius, no shadow; near-black ink, near-black pill primary; amber only on data (Google bars, the current step), green only for done states; Geist, tabular figures.
STORY: The owner sees the next step or the result in five seconds, acts in one tap (ask on WhatsApp, renew, reply to a note), and trusts the numbers because zeros are zeros.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Title row (Overview + one-line finding, "Ask a customer" pill right). Four-cell launch track spanning the sheet.
