What we will never do.

Reputra is built around Google's review rules. If a business wants any of these broken, Reputra is not for that business.

  • Hide the Google button from unhappy customers.Every customer sees it, happy or not.
  • Offer gifts or discounts for reviews.Not once, not as a "thank you".
  • Write or buy a review.Every review comes from a real visit.
  • Send bulk WhatsApp messages.One at a time, from your own number.

Questions owners ask us.

Is this allowed by Google?

Yes. We only link to Google's own review box, every customer sees the Google button, and we never offer gifts for reviews. Those are Google's rules, and Reputra is built around them.

Does the customer need an app?

No. They hold their phone to the card or scan the QR code, a page opens in their browser, and one more tap opens Google's review box.

What is in the setup pack?

Two counter stands, each with a QR code and a tap spot, and one tap card for the till. We set them up in person and link them to your review page. They are yours to keep, even if you stop.

Does every phone work?

Almost every phone made in the last five years can tap. For the rest, the QR code on the stand and on the back of the card does the same job.

Will my WhatsApp number get banned?

No. Messages go one at a time from your own WhatsApp, sent by your staff to customers who visited. We don't use bulk-sending tools.

What if a customer is unhappy?

They can send you private feedback first, and you get an alert. The Google button stays on their screen too, because hiding it is against Google's rules.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

Raast, JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer, monthly or yearly. Cancel any month, with no fee. The card and the stand stay with you.
